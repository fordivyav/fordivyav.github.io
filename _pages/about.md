Divya Veerapaneni, MD, is a neurology resident at the University of Texas Southwestern adult neurology program. She grew up in Silicon Valley and obtained her medical degree at Boston University as part of their combined seven year accelerated BS/MD program. Prior to medical school, she studied computer science, including graduate level artifical intelligence courses, and did research in a computational medicine research lab. Her research has been published in multiple peer-reviewed journals. When she's not working in the hospital, she loves to play board games, go on nature walks, and engage in social work.
